# Data notices

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the source code only. Check the terms of each data file before you publish or redistribute a copy of this repository.

## LEE Filters — not included

No LEE Filters data ships with this repository, because the licence for redistributing it is unclear. The built-in "Colour gels" are synthetic examples. The app can import LEE spectra you collected yourself (`tools/lee_scraper.py` and `tools/lee_csv_to_json.py` help, check LEE's terms first). If you put them in `data/lee.json` (format in `docs/DATA_FORMATS.md`), `tools/build.py` embeds them in your own build. That file is git-ignored; do not publish it unless LEE permits it.

## `data/gels.json` — synthetic example colour gels

Generated by `tools/make_example_gels.py`. Smooth made-up curves: not measurements and not derived from any manufacturer's data. Same licence as the code.

## `data/colorchecker.json` — ColorChecker reflectance

Spectral reflectance of the 24 ColorChecker patches (Ohta data) exported with `tools/gen_colorchecker.py` from [colour-science](https://www.colour-science.org/) (BSD-3-Clause). Check the original publication's terms before commercial redistribution. "ColorChecker" is a trademark of X-Rite.

## Munsell — not included

Munsell spectra (for example the University of Eastern Finland measurements) are not bundled. Import them yourself (`tools/spectra_matrix_to_csv.py`, see `docs/DATA_FORMATS.md`) and follow the licence of the source you use.

## `data/pigments.json` — artist pigment reflectance spectra

Reflectance spectra of 82 pigments painted in an acrylic binder on cardboard. The project owner has the rights to use and publish this data. The files did not name who measured them; add the credit here and in `src/js/app/datasets/pigments.js` (the `attrib` text) if you know it.

## Colour-matching functions

The CIE 1931 2° standard observer table and the D65 table in `src/js/core/` are the official CIE tables. The CAT matrices are the published ones for each transform.

## `data/standard-sources.json` and `data/colour-tables.json`

Spectral tables of the CIE standard illuminants (F series, LED series, A, D65), the TM-30 colour evaluation samples, the CRI test colour samples, the CIE 1964 10° observer and the D-series basis functions. They are published by the CIE and IES and were exported with `tools/gen_standard_sources.py` and `tools/gen_colour_tables.py` from the Python package [colour-science](https://www.colour-science.org/) (BSD-3-Clause). Check the CIE's terms if you redistribute them separately from this project.

## LED spectra you import

Spectra you import stay in your browser and are not part of this repository. If you share exports from hcri.io or another measurement database, follow that site's terms and credit the people who measured the lights. The files in `examples/leds/` are synthetic.
