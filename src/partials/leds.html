Every light source in one place: the built-in CIE standard sources and your own LED spectra. Each point is one light. Colour the points by CRI or TM-30 to see which parts of the diagram hold good and poor colour rendering, then pick one to see its spectrum and its TM-30 colour vector graphic.

Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, click a point to select it. Only lights that pass the filters on the right are drawn. Lights with no CCT (far from the Planckian locus) are drawn but have no CRI or TM-30 value and appear grey.
